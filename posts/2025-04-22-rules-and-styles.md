> **Note:** inspired by https://stephango.com/style

article style
- Write short and self-contained doc/essay/atomic notes(try to explain a single idea/topic clearly).
- simplicity is virtue. 简洁是一种美德。

file style
- Markdown files as knowledge storage format.
- No more than 3 head level per doc.

task management
- No more than 3 tasks per day.
- Focus session = 3 hrs.

- Learning (every thing) process:
    1. Find: Choose a [concept] you'd like to know about.
    2. Clarify: Write articles to clarify.
    3. Dig: If you encounter confusion in step 2, delve into the challenging idea and apply step 2 to it

keep tracking
- wrote 1 essay to summarize decision you made per week.
    - review outcome and invest.
